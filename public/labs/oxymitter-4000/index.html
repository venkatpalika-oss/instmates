    What this lab models

    Deliberate boundaries

    • Steady signal scaling uses the documented proportional 4–20 mA relationship. Values outside the configured span are withheld; no saturation behavior is invented.
    • Cell mV uses exact entries from Table 8-1 at 736°C and 20.95% reference oxygen. Other concentrations show no table value. No interpolation or fitted cell constant.
    • Time advances by explicit actions. Startup is approximately 30 minutes in the manual, with no thermal ramp modeled. Calibration sampling defaults to 5 minutes; purge defaults to 3 minutes.
    • Calibration has selected valid/invalid results. There is no synthetic slope, constant, degradation model, or proof of real calibration validity.
    • Faults are injected one at a time. No numerical fault thresholds, simultaneous-alarm priority, wiring faults, or repair actions are assumed.
    • Physical behavior such as furnace chemistry, temperature response, reference-air loss, diffusion blockage, gas mixing, and external loop supply failure is outside this model.
    • Controls are learning controls, not a complete LOI, HART, AMS, or membrane-keypad emulator. Xi electronics and other probe/converter variants are excluded.
    Edition caveat: Calibration Recommended

    Table 2-8 and §5.2.2 say this alarm was disabled/discontinued in 2014. Other passages retain historical references. It is not generated here. Logic modes containing it are labeled as legacy references.

    The §3.4.3 text contains a duplicated TP6 reference. This lab uses the consistent TP5/TP6 descriptions in §5.2.3–4; it does not turn that typo into wiring guidance.
